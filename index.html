The Basics

How Ads Bring Parents to Madaras

Step 1

A parent sees the ad

While scrolling Facebook or Instagram, a parent in your area sees a short ad from Madaras.

Step 2

They reach out

They tap the ad, then send a message, call, or fill in a short form.

Step 3

Madaras follows up

Your team replies, shares fees and details, and invites the parent to visit.

Step 4

We improve it

Every week we check which ads bring the most parents, and do more of what works.

Words you will see in this proposal

Meta

The company that owns Facebook and Instagram. Ads on both are bought in one place.

Enquiry

One parent contacting the school after seeing an ad, by message, call or form.

Ad budget

The money Meta charges to show the ads to parents. It is paid to Meta, not to us.

Service 1 — Ads for the 2027 Intake

We Run the Ads. You Get the Enquiries.

What we do
  • Set up the ads on Facebook and Instagram: pictures, videos and wording.
  • Choose who sees them: parents in your area who are likely to be looking for a school.

What We Need From Madaras

Six Things That Help the Ads Work.

Access

Your Facebook and Instagram pages

You add Trance AI as a partner on the pages. Madaras stays the owner and can remove our access at any time. We will guide you through it.

Payment

A Meta ad account with a payment method

This is where Meta collects the ad budget, usually from a bank card. It is opened in Madaras's name, and we can set it up with you.

Goals

Your 2027 intake targets

How many new students you want in each class or level, and the last date for applications.

Information

What parents ask about

Fees or how to request them, curriculum, location, how to apply, open day dates and contact numbers.

Photos

Real photos and videos

Classrooms, activities and events. Photos showing children need the parents' permission, which the school collects.

People

Someone to reply to parents

A phone or WhatsApp number checked every day, and one contact person for quick approvals.

Please share these before the October launch, so the ads can go live on time.
